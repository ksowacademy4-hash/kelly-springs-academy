About Our School

Discover our history, our values, and the people who make Kelly Springs a special place to learn.

Our Vision

To be the leading institution in Owerri for early childhood and primary education, recognized for producing well-rounded, morally upright, and academically excellent future leaders.

Our Mission

To provide a supportive and stimulating environment where children from Toddler to Basic 5 can discover their unique talents. We combine the Federal curriculum with modern teaching methods to ensure every child thrives intellectually, socially, and emotionally.

Our Curriculum

A perfect blend of the Federal curriculum and modern teaching methods, designed to nurture every stage of your child's growth.

🧸

Early Years

Toddler • Reception 1 & 2 • Transition

Our Early Years program focuses on sparking curiosity through structured play, sensory activities, and foundational learning. We build confidence and independence in a warm, safe environment.

  • ✓ Phonics & Early Literacy: Building strong reading and writing foundations.
  • ✓ Numeracy: Introduction to numbers, shapes, and basic problem-solving.
  • ✓ Motor Skills: Arts, crafts, and physical play to develop coordination.
  • ✓ Social Development: Learning to share, communicate, and play together.
🎓

Primary Education

Basic 1 to Basic 5

Strictly aligned with the Federal Ministry of Education curriculum, our Primary section prepares students for national examinations and future academic success through rigorous, engaging lessons.

  • ✓ Core Subjects: Comprehensive English, Mathematics, and Basic Science & Technology.
  • ✓ Humanities: Social Studies, Civic Education, and Cultural Studies.
  • ✓ Critical Thinking: Encouraging independent thought and problem-solving skills.
  • ✓ Assessment: Regular continuous assessments to track and ensure student progress.
